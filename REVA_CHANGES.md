Jason (Response 15): "As you write the gateware, the most important thing is to pay close attention to any
bugs you find or improvements that we should make for revA of the schematic and the PCB. Make a list of such
changes and either do them in parallel, or do them later from your list."

Each entry says what was found, how, what to change, and its state: **open** (to do), **done** (in the
design, with the build that has it), or **checked, no change** (looked at, nothing to do).
