# Rev A: changes found while writing the gateware

Jason (Response 15): "As you write the gateware, the most important thing is to pay close attention to any
bugs you find or improvements that we should make for revA of the schematic and the PCB. Make a list of such
changes and either do them in parallel, or do them later from your list."

Each entry says what was found, how, what to change, and its state: **open** (to do), **done** (in the
design, with the build that has it), or **checked, no change** (looked at, nothing to do).

| # | found | what | change | state |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | gateware, the Icepi's fast sampler | ECP5 2:1 DDR inputs (IDDRX2F, the "GDDRX2" mode PLAN.md section 8 counts on for 1.5 ns resolution) exist only on the left and right banks.  Read from Project Trellis's `LFE5U-25F/iodb.json`: CMP_I (L1), CMP_QP (N1), CMP_QN (P1), CMP_IN (M2) are on bank 6, CMP_IP (J2) and TRIG_IN (G1) on bank 7, all on the left side. | none: every fast input can use IDDRX2F | checked, no change |
| 2 | gateware, the reset state and the power-up order (Response 15) | **The transmitter was on whenever no gateware drove TX_DIS.**  DSOP (the ADL5375's output disable, high = off; "connect to ground or leave floating to enable") hung on a 100 k pull-down (R209), and the board runs from its own USB-C supply: with the host off, unconfigured, booting PYNQ's base overlay or loading another one, DSOP sat low and the modulator ran, the synthesizers still programmed from the last run, with no identification.  A pull-up alone does not cure it: an unpowered host's pin clamps the line at about 0.6 V through its ESD diode, under DSOP's 0.8 V. | Header pin 24 becomes **TX_EN, high = transmit**: U206 (74LVC1G04, beside the modulator) makes DSOP = not TX_EN; R227 1 k holds TX_EN low (a powered-off host's clamp, the Zynq's configuration pull-ups at 10 k or more: 0.35 V at worst, under the inverter's 0.8 V; driven high through the PYNQ's 200 ohm it reads 2.35 V or more, over 2.0 V); R209 becomes a 100 k pull-up on DSOP.  The gateware's CTRL[0] keeps its sense (TX_DIS, reset 1); the tops drive tx_en = not tx_dis. | done (Response 15) |
| 3 | the same review | The synthesizers' and the DSA's latch enables (LE_PLL_TX, LE_PLL_RX, LE_DSA) and the trigger buffer's inputs (TRIG_A, TRIG_B) had no resistor: an undriven header left them floating, so noise on a floating LE could load whatever SCLK / SDATA shifted in, and the TRIG jacks could chatter. | R314-R318, 100 k to ground on the header sheet (each placed beside the pin it holds) | done (Response 15) |
| 4 | the same review | LOOP_SEL (the Costas switch's select when JP103 is on FPGA) floats with no gateware; LOOP_HOLD rests low on its 100 k (the loop closed). | none: LOOP_SEL matters only with JP103 moved to FPGA, and with entry 2 nothing transmits then | checked, no change |
| 5 | the same review | TR_A's 10 k pull-up holds receive while the host is unconfigured, but a powered-off host's clamp pulls TR_A low: the switch turns to SW TX. | none: with entry 2 the modulator is off then, and the LNA is the side kept away from the antenna | checked, no change |
| 6 | gateware, coherent time stamps | Two boards' time stamps could share one time base if the FPGA's sample clock came from the radio's 25 MHz (the receiver's VCXO is locked to the far transmitter's TCXO): one-way time transfer, not only round trips. | none on the board: TRIG_IN is on a clock-capable pin on both hosts (U19 MRCC, G1 PCLK), so a cable from REF OUT to TRIG IN (JP202 open) brings the reference in; the gateware option is in gateware/README.md's "not here yet" | checked, no change |
| 7 | gateware, the samplers | The ISERDESE2 (PYNQ-Z2) at 400 MHz DDR from the clock wizard meets timing (pulse width 0.35 ns spare); the ECP5's IDDRX1F's sample order is from TN1265, unsimulated. | none on the board; BRINGUP.md stage 0.5 checks the Icepi's order with a jumper | checked, no change |
