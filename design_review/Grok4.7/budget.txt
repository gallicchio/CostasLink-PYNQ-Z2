== 2c. The USB-C amplifier modules Jason has (datasheets/AliExpress*.pdf, Prompt 8)
PA module: SBB5089+SE5004 5.8 GHz 2 W module, 40 dB, P1dB +33 dBm, 2 A at 5 V.  To run it at +30 dBm it wants -10 dBm in: the board's +9.8 dBm through a 20 dB SMA attenuator gives +29.8 dBm out.  Without the pad the module is driven 17 dB into compression.  Heat: about 9 W in a 45 x 30 x 14 mm shell: a heatsink or a fan, and a 5 V / 3 A source (a USB-C PD bank in the field).
LNA module (assumed numbers): 15 dB, NF 2.0 dB ahead of a 10 m cable (5 dB) and this board (NF 1.5): system NF 2.2 dB; the DSA takes the 15 dB of extra gain.  Its filter is the receiver's only selectivity before the demodulator: with the own transmitter 600 MHz away it adds the 30 dB (assumed) that section 4 asks of the antennas.

== 4. One antenna?  TX-to-RX isolation needed for frequency-division duplex
arm filter 100 MHz, 2 poles; the wanted signal at 10 m is -53.9 dBm
  TX/RX   25 MHz apart: filter rejects  0.5 dB, leakage at the RX port must be below -63.3 dBm => 73 dB of isolation
  TX/RX   50 MHz apart: filter rejects  1.9 dB, leakage at the RX port must be below -61.9 dBm => 72 dB of isolation
  TX/RX  100 MHz apart: filter rejects  6.0 dB, leakage at the RX port must be below -57.9 dBm => 68 dB of isolation
  TX/RX  200 MHz apart: filter rejects 14.0 dB, leakage at the RX port must be below -49.9 dBm => 60 dB of isolation
  TX/RX  600 MHz apart: filter rejects 31.4 dB, leakage at the RX port must be below -32.5 dBm => 42 dB of isolation
  a splitter gives 3 dB, a circulator about 20 dB, two patches side by side 30-40 dB: FDD on one antenna does not work in-band;
  separate TX and RX antennas, or time-division with a switch (the protocol is half duplex anyway).

== 6. The Costas loop (the RX synthesizer's VCXO is the loop's VCO)
PLL multiplies the 25 MHz VCXO by N = 232; pull +-100 ppm = +-580 kHz at 5.8 GHz over 2.7 V: Ko = 2.70e+06 rad/s/V
Kd = 0.25 V/rad at the DSA's regulated level (-20 dBm at the demodulator, kept from 3 m to about 300 m); for fn = 3000 Hz, zeta = 0.71 with C = 100 nF:
  R1 = 18.9 k (E24 18.0 k), R2 = 750 ohm (E24 750)
  pull-in from 30 kHz off: 3.8 ms (the FPGA steps the RX PLL when the VCXO cannot reach)
  pull-in from 300 kHz off: 375.2 ms (the FPGA steps the RX PLL when the VCXO cannot reach)
  two TCXO-referenced transmitters differ by at most 29 kHz; the VCXO reaches +-580 kHz
