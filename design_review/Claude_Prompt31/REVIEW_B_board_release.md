# CostasLink-PYNQ-Z2 rev A: PCB and manufacturing-package review (reviewer B, 2026-10-14)

**What was reviewed.** The board file at git HEAD (a261e2c: `hardware/CostasLink-PYNQ-Z2.kicad_pcb`, 418 footprints,
4205 track segments, 1088 vias, 13 zones) and the committed release `mfg/release/` of 2026-10-12 (gerbers, drills,
`BOM_JLCPCB.csv`, the two CPLs, `PLACEMENT_CHECKS.txt`, `FOOTPRINT_CHECK.txt`, `ORDER_SETTINGS.md`, `CONSIGNMENT.md`),
plus `build/route_report.json` and the renders.  Nothing in the project tree was written or run; KiCad was not launched.
The board was parsed as text (`parse_board.py`) and measured with shapely/scipy (`analyze.py`, `analyze2.py`,
`analyze3.py`; their outputs `analysis*.txt` are beside this file).  The TDK HHM1907B1 question is answered from the
footprint source (`gen/footprints.py`) and the datasheet text, since the committed board still carries the Johanson.

**Coordinates** below are the gerber/CPL frame: mm from the board's bottom-left corner, x right, **y up** (the aux
origin at page (25, 125)).  `gen/placement.py` counts y *down* from the top-left: y_placement = 100 - y.

**Standard applied.** CLAUDE_PCB.md sections 5, 6, 8, 10, 14 (RF) and 15; `sim/rf_lines.py` (CPWG 0.29/0.25 on
0.2104 mm of 7628: 49.8 ohm, eeff 3.13, lambda 29.2 mm at 5.8 GHz, lambda/10 2.92 mm, quarter wave 7.30 mm);
JLCPCB's 4-layer capability as the repository records it (section 8: 0.15 mm track/gap designed, dam 0.10 mm at 1 oz).

## 1. Findings, ranked

No BLOCKING finding.  The board is electrically whole (every net one copper cluster, GND included, measured
independently of KiCad's DRC), the package is consistent, and nothing found would stop assembly or the first power-up.
The SERIOUS items are RF performance and supply-feed robustness, each fixable in the generator at no fabrication cost.

## 2. Checked and found right

RF (section 14, `sim/rf_lines.py`):
- The 27 RF-class and 12 LO-class nets: every track on F.Cu except the RX coupler's ISO port (M2); 0.29 mm lines, 0.25 mm necks into the narrow pads (0.60 mm), 0.20 mm for the first 1.4 mm out of the QFN pins (58.6 ohm). No via on any chain. No dangling stub: the four ends the checker flagged are the ESD-shunt branches (L101, L202, L401) meeting the line with overlapped copper.
- CPWG gap: the top ground fill sits 0.250 mm from every RF/LO track edge (79 segments of 0.3 mm or more: min 0.250, max 1.256 where a pad pushes it off). Z0 49.8 ohm, eeff 3.13, lambda 29.2 mm; 0.20 mm = 58.6, 0.25 mm = 53.3 ohm.
- In1 under every RF/LO line (line plus gaps plus 0.3 mm): 10.6 mm2 missing in all, 8.4 mm2 of it the SMA pins' antipads (S1), the rest 0.07-0.22 mm2 nibbles at other nets' vias (M11). The plane is one polygon, 92.4 % of the board.
- Fence on the RX chain, TX chain and the switch's three lines: vias at 1.2-1.75 mm both sides (lambda/17 or better); 16.9 mm switch lines carry 13-15 vias a side.
- LO legs: RX 10.76 / 10.76 mm, TX 11.76 / 11.76 mm (three nets each through two DC blocks: C149/C119, C150/C120, C229/C204, C230/C205): 0.000 mm skew. P to LOIN, N to LOIP as the response says.
- Couplers CP101 (24.0 and 24.54, y 46.75-54.05) and CP201 (55.46 and 56.0, y 80.85-88.15): two 7.30 mm lines (= lambda/4 at eeff 3.13), 0.29 wide, 0.250 mm edge gap, no ground fill between them (0.000 mm2), 0.29 mm square pads; both ISO ports terminated in 49.9 R (R103, R213), the TX one on top in 4.2 mm of 0.29.
- DC blocks in every chain where the netlist puts them; the ESD shunts L101/L202/L401 are on the jack side of the block with 2.4 / 1.0 / 1.1 mm branches (0.08 lambda at most: 6.8 nH + branch transforms to about -j200 ohm: 0.07 dB).

## 4. Verdict

The board as drawn will assemble and will power up.  Every net is a single piece of copper by an independent
measurement; the 5 V, 3.3 V and ground paths are whole, the exposed pads are gridded with vias, the paste and mask are
sane, the BOM/CPL/gerber set agrees with the board and with the order form, and the package names the consigned parts,
the hand-soldered socket and the parts over the edge.  What it will not do as drawn is meet its own RF standard at the
jacks: the five RF SMA launches each reflect about 12 % of the power (return loss 9 dB, 0.6 dB) because the pin keeps its
2.0 mm rings on the inner layers inside 2.5 mm antipads, and the RF and VCO supply pins see their capacitors through
3.5-7.7 mm of track.  Those, and the single 0.25 mm vias that carry the whole 1.4 A into and out of the +5V_IN and +5V_RF
pours, are generator changes that cost nothing at JLCPCB and are worth a build before ordering; nothing else here should
delay the order.
