## 3. Would this board power up and work?

Power would come up: the OR-ing, the five LDOs, the TVS and the loads are consistent (1.37 A at 5 V, ≥ 4.89 V on
+5V_RF), nothing fights on the header, the synthesizers, the DSA and the modulator rest in safe states (DSA at
31.75 dB, TX off, LE lines low), and every chip's pin map matches its datasheet.  But as committed the receiver
would not work: C154, C155, C156 and C157 (100 nF each) are wired to the THS4541 fast-arm outputs, the loop
op amp's output and the sign switch instead of to the ADS1115's pins, so the 100 MHz I and Q arms are shorted
to ground above a few kilohertz (the THS4541 cannot drive 100 nF: its datasheet calls that unstable, and the
output can supply 0.1 A where a 0.5 V / 3.5 ns step into 100 nF asks 14 A), the Costas error is half missing,
the VC node rings, and the same drawing error puts 100 nF on the AD8310 and the peak-hold buffer on sheet 6.
That is one line in `gen/sch_rx.py` and one in `gen/sch_slow.py` (the capacitor at `px − 2.54`, not
`x0 + 2.54`) plus re-placing six 0402s.  Independently of that, the ADL5380's RF inputs are tied to the balun
with no blocking capacitors where ADI's text, Figure 81 and evaluation board all put 100 pF; if the HHM1907B1
has a DC path from a balanced terminal to its GND terminal (the usual lattice structure; TDK publishes no
circuit), RFIN sits at 0 V and the receiver has no gain — two 0402 capacitors settle it either way.  With A1-A3
fixed I see nothing else in the schematic that would make a board dead on arrival or a measurement wrong by
more than the 0.5 dB of A4; the TX chain, the synthesizers, the comparators and the power are right by the
numbers.

## 4. Against the earlier reviews (read last)

New here: **A1, A2** (the ADC filter capacitors on the source side of the 10 k: no earlier report mentions
C154-C157, C524, C525 or capacitive loading of the THS4541/OPA2388/AD8310), **A3** (the missing AC coupling
between the balun and RFIP/RFIN: the first round checked the balun's footprint and terminal numbers, not the
ADL5380's coupling requirement), **A4** (the doubled 1.5 pF blocks; Grok4.7 priced a single 1.5 pF at
0.5 dB), **A6**, **A7**, **A9**.  Already known and closed by the response: the TCXO's pin 1 (A5 here is only a
request for a footprint, the first round's reading "E/D with internal pull-up" stands), Kd = one leg (R126
10 k, done), the common-mode margin into the THS4541 (61 mV, asserted), the ADL5375 bias divider, TX_EN /
DSOP, the LE pull-downs, the LO pair crossing, the switch-port limits.  A8 restates REVA_CHANGES #4.
